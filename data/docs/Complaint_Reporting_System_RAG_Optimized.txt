Council Complaint Reporting and Service Management System – User Knowledge Base (RAG Optimized)
System Overview

The Council Complaint Reporting and Service Management System is a digital platform that allows residents to report municipal service-related issues. The system ensures complaints are recorded, reviewed, and resolved in a structured and transparent manner.

Complaint Categories

Users must select one of the following categories when submitting a complaint:

1. Roads & Transport

Issues related to potholes, damaged roads, faded road markings, broken traffic lights, blocked streets, traffic congestion, or public transport-related concerns.

2. Sanitation & Waste

Issues related to missed garbage collection, illegal dumping, overflowing bins, waste management problems, or uncollected refuse.

3. Water & Sewage

Issues related to burst pipes, water leaks, sewage blockages, drainage problems, water shortages, or contaminated water.

4. Electricity

Issues related to power outages, faulty streetlights, exposed electrical wiring, transformer faults, or unstable electricity supply.

5. Other

Any municipal service issue that does not clearly fall into the above categories.

Complaint Submission Methods

Users can submit complaints through:

The web application

WhatsApp chatbot

Complaints may be submitted anonymously or with personal details.

WhatsApp Reporting

When reporting via WhatsApp, users are guided step-by-step through a chat conversation. The chatbot may request:

Category selection

Description of the issue

Location details

Optional contact information

Users may attach supporting evidence such as images, videos, or voice notes. Once submitted, the complaint is logged into the system for review.

Complaint Processing Stages

Submitted complaints move through the following stages:

Pending – Complaint has been received

In Review – Complaint is being assessed

Resolved – Action has been taken

Rejected – Complaint is invalid, incomplete, or outside system scope

Anonymous Reporting

Users may choose to submit complaints anonymously. Personal details are optional unless the user wishes to receive feedback or track their complaint.

User Rights and Responsibilities

Users have the right to:

Confidential handling of their complaint

Fair assessment

Respectful communication

Users must avoid:

Submitting false information

Abusive or offensive content

Duplicate complaints

Data Privacy and Security

All complaint data is securely stored. Personal information, if provided, is protected and accessible only to authorized personnel. WhatsApp data is handled according to applicable platform privacy policies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit a complaint anonymously?
Yes. You may choose not to provide personal details when submitting a complaint.

How long does it take to resolve a complaint?
Resolution time varies depending on the category and complexity of the issue.

Can I submit evidence?
Yes. Images, videos, and voice notes can be attached, especially when using WhatsApp.

What happens after I submit a complaint?
Your complaint is recorded, assigned a category, and reviewed by administrators.

Can I track my complaint?
Tracking is available if you provide contact details or receive a reference ID.

Support and Assistance

For help using the system, users should contact the system administrator.

This platform is not intended for emergency situations. For emergencies, users should contact the appropriate emergency services.
